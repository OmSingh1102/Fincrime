How the gate moves the split

Full-date conflicts of ten years or more clear at any gate up to 96; the five-to-nine-year group drops out above 88; year-only conflicts above 92 or 85. An exact date of birth never clears a record, and a sanctions record never clears while the guard is on. At 97 nothing clears, and the whole book goes to people - the before-and-after number is in the flash after each re-run.
